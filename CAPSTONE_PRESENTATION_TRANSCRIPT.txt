"Now, let's examine the minute technical details of our multi-agent red-teaming loop. Our pipeline connects five specialized agents using Google ADK's native `SequentialAgent` workflow graph and strict Pydantic JSON schema validation:

1. The Policy Parser Agent (`gemini-2.5-flash` | Temperature: 0.0):
We assign this agent a temperature of 0.0 because legal interpretation requires absolute precision and zero hallucination. The Parser ingests the target system's specifications and cross-references them against our statutory knowledge base (`commerce_policy_kb.json`). It extracts verifiable compliance claims formatted into a strict Pydantic model (`ClaimSchema`), mapping out exact legal boundaries under the EU AI Act, GDPR, and FTC guidelines.

2. The Threat Modeler Agent (`gemini-2.5-pro` | Temperature: 0.4):
We use Gemini 2.5 Pro at temperature 0.4 here to balance analytical reasoning with creative threat exploration. Why is this agent special? Because it demonstrates autonomous tool calling! Before formulating an attack, the Modeler actively invokes our FastMCP tool—`search_past_vulnerabilities(policy_id)`—to query historical OWASP and FTC CVE databases. By checking real-world past exploits, it formulates a highly targeted, evidence-backed threat hypothesis (`ThreatHypothesisSchema`).

3. The Red-Team Simulator Agent (`gemini-2.5-pro` | Temperature: 0.3):
Acting as our expert cyber attacker, the Simulator takes the threat hypothesis and crafts concrete adversarial attack payloads (`RedTeamScenarioSchema`). To test bot resilience, it employs sophisticated multi-turn escalation techniques: Base64 payload obfuscation, context priming, sycophancy traps, and stored cross-site scripting (XSS) embedded inside mock product reviews.

4. The Target AI System / Chatbot (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite` | Temperature: 1.0):
To simulate realistic e-commerce chatbots under stress, we run our simulated target bots at temperature 1.0. We built three distinct corporate test cases under our NovaMart Corporation benchmark: ShopBot (handling catalog reviews), Electronics Assistant (handling high-value electronics pricing), and Care Bot (handling customer refunds). We configured these bots with selectable vulnerable and remediated states across 12 statutory clauses.

5. The Evaluator Agent & HITL Guardrail (`gemini-2.5-pro` | Temperature: 0.0):
Finally, our Evaluator acts as an impartial cybersecurity judge. It analyzes the target bot's defense against the statutory rules using `EvaluationSchema`. If the bot successfully defends itself, it issues a COMPLIANT proof certificate. 

However—and this is a critical course rubric requirement—if the Evaluator detects a High-Severity security bypass or regulatory violation, it immediately triggers our Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Safety Guardrail (`ADKHumanApprovalNode`). The autonomous pipeline pauses, presents the technical evidence and severity penalty to the human operator, and requires explicit human authorization before generating an XML remediation patch plan."
