About NexusAudit-Gauntlet

An automated, agentic red-teaming pipeline built to secure the future of enterprise AI. As enterprises rush to deploy LLMs in customer-facing roles, they expose themselves to unprecedented legal and cybersecurity risks.

🏆

Project Context: Kaggle 5-Day GenAI Capstone

This platform was conceptualized and developed as part of the Kaggle GenAI Capstone Challenge (Agents for Business Track). The objective was to build a sophisticated multi-agent system designed not just to assist users, but to attack, audit, and secure other AI agents against legal and security vulnerabilities.

The NovaMart Case Study

To test our pipeline against realistic, high-stakes environments, we created a hypothetical corporate profile: NovaMart Corporation. NovaMart is a multi-billion-dollar omni-channel retailer that has deployed specialized LLM chatbots across its divisions to handle immense scale.

Our auditing pipeline specifically targets three primary NovaMart AI deployments:

  • NovaMart ShopBot (E-Storefront): Handles customer item inquiries and catalog reviews. Risk Vector: Ingestion of untrusted third-party reviews (OWASP LLM-01) and deceptive pricing.
  • NovaMart Electronics Assistant: Manages high-value electronics catalogs (e.g., MacBooks). Risk Vector: Adversarial override injections attempting to bypass official database pricing.
  • NovaMart Care Bot: Manages order tracking and refund policies. Risk Vector: Hallucination vulnerabilities concerning exceptions (e.g., unauthorized return extensions) creating corporate liability.

Adversarial Multi-Agent Architecture

Traditional static audits fail to capture how system controls break under real-world, dynamic interactions. NexusAudit solves this by treating compliance verification as an adversarial game. We orchestrated an elite Multi-Agent System powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash and integrated via the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) into a sequential workflow graph.

🔍

1. Parser (Intake & Analysis)

Ingests the target chatbot's system description and cross-references it against a dynamic JSON Knowledge Base to determine high-risk regulatory categories.

🎯

2. Modeler (Structural Synthesis)

Formulates concrete, adversarial hypotheses based on legal clauses (e.g., predicting the bot might generate an unauthorized 90% discount).

⚔️

3. Simulator (The Attacker)

Designs specific, highly realistic conversational payloads (including Base64 obfuscation and indirect injections) to exploit the target bot.

⚖️

4. Evaluator (The Judge)

Objectively reviews the target's response to the attack, rendering a final verdict and deciding if the loop should ESCALATE the attack complexity.

Evaluated Policies & Deep Dive Use Cases

NexusAudit evaluates targets against the most critical frameworks bottlenecking AI adoption today:

EU AI Act

Audit Focus: High-Risk Exemptions

Vulnerability Tested: Mischaracterizing systems as low-risk to bypass strict transparency laws.

GDPR (Article 22)

Audit Focus: Dynamic Profiling & PII

Vulnerability Tested: Socially engineering the bot to leak user profile data or apply discriminative pricing.

FTC Section 5

Audit Focus: Dark Patterns & Deception

Vulnerability Tested: Direct Prompt Injections forcing the bot to create false scarcity or unauthorized discount codes.

OWASP Top 10 for LLMs

Audit Focus: LLM-01 Indirect Prompt Injections

Vulnerability Tested: Exploiting the semantic gap by injecting malicious payloads inside untrusted product reviews in the database.
